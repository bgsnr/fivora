## Rationale

Pitch is a professional presentation workspace competing in a crowded SaaS market. The design system projects sophistication and creative ambition through a bold, high-contrast visual language. The deep primary color (#0c021c, nearly black) paired with a vibrant purple surface (#5318eb) and true blue accent (#0000ee) creates dramatic visual separation and signals premium positioning.

## 1. Visual Theme & Craftsmanship Rules
- **Anti-Slop Guardrails**: Zero em-dashes (`—`), zero generic 3-card equal feature rows, no artificial purple gradients or floating blobs. Keep backgrounds crisp and pure (#ffffff) with deep primary (#0c021c) and surgical purple highlights (#5318eb).
- **Typography Execution**: Extreme scale differentiation with Mark Pro / bold display hierarchies. No mixed random serifs.
- **Dial Calibration**: DESIGN_VARIANCE: 7, MOTION_INTENSITY: 6, VISUAL_DENSITY: 4.
- **Component & CTA Discipline**: Single verb per action, no duplicate CTA intent, WCAG AA verified contrast ratios.
